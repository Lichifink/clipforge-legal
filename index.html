ClipForge

ClipForge es una herramienta personal de escritorio para Windows. Corre en la computadora de su dueño y sirve para editar videos largos en clips verticales (subtítulos, formato, comentarios grabados por su dueño) y publicarlos en las cuentas propias de su dueño en YouTube, TikTok e Instagram, usando solo las APIs oficiales de cada plataforma.

No es un servicio público: no hay cuentas de usuario, no se vende y no se ofrece a terceros. Solo publica en las cuentas que su dueño conecta y autoriza explícitamente.

English

ClipForge is a personal desktop tool for Windows. It runs on its owner's computer, turns long videos into vertical clips (captions, layout, commentary recorded by its owner) and publishes them to its owner's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts, using only each platform's official APIs.

It is not a public service: there are no user accounts, it is not sold or offered to anyone else. It only publishes to the accounts its owner connects and explicitly authorizes.
